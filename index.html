How I Work

My design workflow blends logical problem-solving with deliberate visual communication. By developing a diverse range of projects, from interactive games to complex data dashboards and e-commerce platforms, my approach has evolved into a comprehensive methodology for building robust, user-centric web applications using modern technologies like React, Vite, and TailwindCSS.

Visual & Verbal Synergy

A core pillar of my workflow is establishing a strong aesthetic and communicative synergy tailored to each project's domain. I believe that an interface should speak the user's language, both visually and verbally. For instance, when designing the Antiquity Archives e-commerce platform, I adopted an "Expert Curator" persona and utilized historically resonant typography and parchment-like textures to build trust and authenticity.

Conversely, for data-heavy applications like the Music Evolution Dashboard, I draw inspiration from the strict constructivist principles of the 1920s Bauhaus movement. By focusing on the "3Cs" (Context, Clutter-Free layouts, and high Contrast) and relying on strict geometric grids and primary colour palettes, I create high-fidelity interfaces that prioritize data clarity without sacrificing visual impact.

Usability & Cognitive Psychology

Usability and cognitive psychology are embedded in every step of my prototyping process. I actively apply Gestalt Principles of Perception, such as Closure, Continuity, Similarity, and Proximity, to guide how users naturally group and interpret information. This was central to the mechanics of my Steel Shark memory game, where visual pattern recognition drives the entire user experience.

Furthermore, my interfaces are rigorously audited against Nielsen's Usability Heuristics. Whether it's implementing defensive error prevention and persistent status trackers in complex checkout flows, or maintaining consistency across bilingual dashboards, my ultimate goal is to build digital experiences that are intuitive. I often leverage wireframing tools like Figma and AI coding assistants to quickly scaffold components, allowing me to dedicate more time to refining these critical user interactions.
