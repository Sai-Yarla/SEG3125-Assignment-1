About Me

Hello! I'm Sai Yarlagadda, a Computer Science student at the University of Ottawa. Outside of my studies, I've gained valuable professional experience as a co-op student at CIBC the past few summers. My passion lies at the intersection of logical problem-solving and creative design. When I'm not coding or designing, you can usually find me heavily invested in hockey, or obsessively updating my Letterboxd with my latest movie reviews.

How I Work

I'm still pretty new to the world of UI/UX design, but I'm having a lot of fun learning the core elements and visual communication concepts in my university classes.

Following advice from resources like the Interaction Design Foundation, I understand that showing how you work is just as important as what you make. Since my workflow is still taking shape, my current approach is all about soaking up knowledge.

Reading up on usability guidelines from leaders like the Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g), I'm gathering feedback, and constantly tweaking my designs. As I progress, I’m excited to expand my toolkit, dive into real user testing, and get hands-on with prototyping.
